## Why missed-call text-back

Any busy front desk misses a call now and then, and Northview Dental Studio was no different. Missed-call text-back makes sure the caller still hears from you.

## What I did

- Set up **missed-call text-back** through Flowa AI, so callers who can't get through get a text right away
- Patients can **reply to the text** instead of calling back
