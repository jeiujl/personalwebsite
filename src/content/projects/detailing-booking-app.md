## The problem

Every booking happened over text: what packages do you offer, how much, are you free Saturday, what's the address? The owner spent hours each week answering the same questions instead of working.

## What I did

- Mapped every question customers asked into a simple flow: choose a package, pick a time, add your address, pay.
- Designed the screens in Figma with large tap targets, clear prices and photos of each package.
- Built a clickable prototype and watched real customers try it, then fixed the spots where they hesitated.
- Handed off a complete design file, ready for a developer to build.

## The result

A design the owner could show investors and developers on day one, and a booking flow simple enough that customers didn't need instructions.
