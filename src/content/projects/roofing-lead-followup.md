## The problem

The owner was on job sites all day. When the phone rang and nobody answered, most callers simply called the next roofer on Google. Leads were tracked in a notebook, and follow-up depended on someone remembering.

## What I did

- Set up the company's own CRM, branded with their logo, with a simple pipeline: New lead → Contacted → Estimate booked → Won.
- Turned on missed-call text-back, so every missed caller gets a friendly text within seconds.
- Built a follow-up sequence that keeps checking in by text and email until the lead replies or books.
- Added online booking for estimates, with automatic reminders to cut no-shows.
- Trained the team in a 30-minute call and gave them the app on their phones.

## The result

Every lead gets a response, even at 7pm. The owner can see the whole pipeline at a glance, and follow-up happens on its own.
