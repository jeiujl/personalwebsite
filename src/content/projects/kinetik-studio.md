## The brief

Kinetik is a boutique pilates and mobility studio. Small studios often take bookings through texts, DMs and clunky generic apps. Members can't see how many spots are left, and full classes turn into a long back-and-forth.

## My approach

- **Calm, not clinical:** soft sand and clay neutrals with one bright blue for anything you can tap.
- **Spots left at a glance:** every class card shows how many spots are left before you open it.
- **Boutique, not gym chain:** an elegant serif for headlines next to a clean sans for the details.
- **Light and dark mode:** both built from the same set of colours.

## What's inside

- A **weekly schedule** with filters for reformer, mat and mobility
- A **class page and one-tap booking**, ending in a confirmation with **Add to calendar**
- **Membership and credits**, with plan options
- A **progress ring** for classes attended this month
- An **automatic waitlist**, plus a small **design system**
