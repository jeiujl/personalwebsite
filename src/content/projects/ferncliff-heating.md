## The brief

Ferncliff Heating & Air is a residential HVAC company in west Toronto. Its old site had one services page, so it rarely showed up when people searched for help in their own neighbourhood, and web inquiries sat in an inbox until someone got back from a job.

## My approach

- **Start from what people search:** a keyword map of real demand decides which pages exist, like furnace repair in Etobicoke or heat pumps in Mimico.
- **Useful pages, not filler:** each page answers that neighbourhood's questions, with clear prices, booking and schema markup that search engines and AI assistants can read.
- **Every lead gets a reply in seconds:** forms and missed calls trigger a friendly text with a booking link, and follow-ups stop once they book.
- **Know what's working:** a monthly report shows which pages and searches bring in booked jobs.

## What's inside

- A **keyword map** grouping search terms by service and neighbourhood
- **Service and area pages** with booking, reviews and an areas-we-serve list
- **Instant text follow-up** on the phone, from form to booked visit
- A **monthly report** of leads and bookings by page
