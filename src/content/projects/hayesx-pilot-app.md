## About HayesX

HayesX makes the HayesX-250, a single-seat electric personal aircraft. The pilot companion app is an app designed for HayesX-250 pilots.

## What I did

- **Designed** the app's screens and flows.
- **Built** it as a progressive web app with Next.js, so it runs in the browser and can be added to a phone's home screen.
- **Managed the project** from outline to a clickable version.

## What's inside

- A **preflight checklist**
- A **digital logbook**
- **Manuals** in one place
- **Messages** and an **account** section
