## The problem

A small team was drowning in a shared inbox: maintenance requests, rent questions, vendor invoices and the occasional real emergency, all mixed together. Important messages sat unread for a day.

## What I did

- Built an automation using Claude AI that reads every incoming email and sorts it into clear categories.
- Maintenance requests are pulled into the team's tracker automatically, with the address and issue filled in.
- For common questions, the assistant drafts a reply so staff just review and send.
- Anything urgent (leaks, lockouts, no heat) triggers an instant text to the on-call manager.

## The result

The team starts each day with a sorted inbox and drafted replies instead of a wall of email, and emergencies get flagged in minutes.
