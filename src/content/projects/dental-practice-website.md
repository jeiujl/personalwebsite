## The problem

The practice's old website looked out of date, was painful to use on a phone, and buried the most important thing, booking an appointment, three clicks deep. Most new patients called during business hours, and calls that came in while the front desk was busy were simply lost.

## What I did

- Interviewed the owner and front desk to find the questions patients ask most (hours, insurance, emergencies) and put the answers on the homepage.
- Designed a clean, friendly layout in Figma, starting from the phone screen, since most visitors arrive on mobile.
- Built the site to load in about a second, with a "Book now" button that stays visible on every page.
- Connected online booking straight to the practice's calendar, with confirmation texts.

## The result

Patients can now book in under a minute, any time of day. The front desk spends less time on the phone, and the practice finally has a site it's proud to send people to.
