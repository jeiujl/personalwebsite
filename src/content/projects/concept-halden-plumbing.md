## The brief

A self-initiated concept for a fictional family-run plumbing and heating company. Most plumber websites make people work too hard. The phone number is buried, prices are a mystery, and booking means leaving a voicemail and hoping for a call back.

## My approach

- **Two clear paths on every page:** book a visit online, or call the 24/7 line. Nothing else competes with them.
- **A warm, trustworthy look:** deep pine green with a copper accent, and a soft serif for headlines that feels local rather than corporate.
- **Answer the worries up front:** fixed prices, arrival windows and the "tidy promise" sit right next to the buttons.
- **Mobile first:** urgent visits often start on a phone, so the call button never scrolls away.

## What's inside

- A **homepage** built around the headline "Fixed fast. Left tidy."
- A **three-step booking flow:** choose a service, pick a time, confirm
- A **services page** with starting prices
- A **service-area map** with a postal code check
- A **sticky call bar** on mobile, plus a small **brand board** with the logo, palette and type
