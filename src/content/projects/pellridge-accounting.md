## The brief

Pellridge Accounting is a small firm with about a dozen staff. Know-how lived in a few senior people's heads and a folder of old checklists, so new staff asked the same questions all day. Onboarding a new client meant weeks of emails asking for the same documents.

## My approach

- **Write the firm down once:** services, fees, processes, document checklists, the client FAQ and the way the firm talks, organised in one place.
- **Answers with sources:** staff ask a question and get an answer that cites the checklist or process it came from, so they can trust it.
- **Agents that know the firm:** every agent reads the Business Brain first, so its emails and answers sound like Pellridge and follow its rules.
- **Polite, persistent onboarding:** each new client gets a checklist, and the agent drafts reminders until every document is in. Staff approve each one.

## What's inside

- The **Business Brain**: an organised, editable knowledge base with last-updated dates
- **Ask the Brain**: a staff chat that answers with citations
- An **onboarding tracker** for each new client: documents received, what's missing and the next reminder
- **Drafted reminder emails** waiting for approval, plus an activity log
